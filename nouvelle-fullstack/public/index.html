our story

it started with two people & one dream

Nouvelle by AK began with Anousha and Khurram, two people who shared more than just a love for each other — they shared a vision of creating something meaningful together. What started with late-night conversations, ideas, and a shared appreciation for fashion slowly grew into the dream of building a brand that felt truly personal.

They wanted Nouvelle to be more than just another fashion label. They imagined pieces that feel effortless, expressive, confident, and beautifully made — clothing that lets people feel like the best version of themselves without trying too hard.

Every idea, every detail, and every collection comes from their shared perspective: Anousha's creative eye and Khurram's vision coming together as one. Nouvelle is a reflection of their journey, their taste, their ambition, and the belief that when two people build something together, it becomes more than a brand — it becomes a story.

This is their dream, their beginning, and just the first chapter of Nouvelle by AK. ✦
